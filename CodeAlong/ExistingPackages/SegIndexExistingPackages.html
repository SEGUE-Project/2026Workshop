Data

We are using open data from the Dutch statistics office CBS, and in particular 500x500 grid cells of Rotterdam. This is count-data coming from the Dutch register, as the 500m x 500m resolution (Statistiek (CBS), 2026). The data was clipped to fit the Rotterdam functional area as defined by the OECD (OECD, 2023). Municipal boundaries come from the kadaster (Kadaster, 2026).

Entropy (Clémentine)

library(OasisR)
library(divseg)
library(segregation)


group_data <- as.data.frame(rotterdam2025) |>
  mutate(share_under25 = (age_0_15 + age_15_25) / population,
         share_25_65 = (age_25_45 + age_45_65) / population ) |>
  select(share_under25, share_25_65, share_older65) |>
  filter(share_under25 > 0 & share_25_65 > 0 & share_older65 > 0)

 entropy <- HTheil(group_data)
